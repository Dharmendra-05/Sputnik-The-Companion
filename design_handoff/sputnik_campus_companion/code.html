Gangotri Girls Hostel (GH) at ABV-IIITM Gwalior provides residential accommodation equipped with 24/7 security, high-speed campus LAN/Wi-Fi, and resident warden supervision [doc_142]. Rooms are allocated on twin-sharing for junior batches and single occupancy for senior scholars. Entry timings stipulate reporting to the hostel turnstiles by 21:30 hrs unless formal academic or lab extension permissions have been authorized by the Council of Wardens [doc_143].

Dining facilities include an attached mess operational for breakfast (07:30–09:30), lunch (12:30–14:15), evening snacks (17:00–18:00), and dinner (19:45–21:45). Common room facilities offer indoor badminton courts, reading lounges, dedicated laundry zones with automated washing units, and immediate medical on-call access to the Institute Health Centre.
